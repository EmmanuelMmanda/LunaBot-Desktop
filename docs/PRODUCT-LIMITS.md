# Product limits and trading risk

LunaBot is a desktop tool for market observation, strategy evaluation, order workflows and account records. Trading involves substantial risk. Software checks do not remove market, broker, network, terminal or execution risk.

- A backtest replays historical data and assumptions; it does not predict or guarantee future results.
- Paper data is synthetic. Demo and Live results are separate broker environments.
- A quote, chart or strategy observation can be stale or incomplete. Check its state and time before use.
- Broker-held positions and pending orders can remain active when the computer or LunaBot is offline.
- Live activation and every submitted order remain subject to account permission, strategy rules, risk limits, broker checks and explicit user settings.
- Trial expiry blocks new exposure and keeps account records and position management available. It does not forcibly close a broker position.
- MCP access is limited to the accounts and permissions granted to each connection. Review and revoke grants you no longer need.

No profitability, broker certification, investment advice or independent audit is claimed.
