# Product limits and trading risk

LunaBot is a desktop tool for market observation, strategy evaluation, order workflows and account records. Trading involves substantial risk. Software checks do not remove market, broker, network, terminal or execution risk.

- A backtest replays historical data and assumptions; it does not predict or guarantee future results.
- Paper data is synthetic. Demo and Live results are separate broker environments.
- A quote, chart or strategy observation can be stale or incomplete. Check its state and time before use.
- Broker-held positions and pending orders can remain active when the computer or LunaBot is offline.
- Live activation and every submitted order remain subject to account permission, strategy rules, risk limits, broker checks and explicit user settings.
- Licence expiry blocks new exposure and keeps account records and position management available. It does not forcibly close a broker position.
- MCP access is limited to the accounts and permissions granted to each connection. Review and revoke grants you no longer need.

No profitability, broker certification, investment advice or independent audit is claimed.

## The 1.0 release candidate

The 1.0.1-rc.22 pilot build is a release candidate, not production certification. Its 28 E28 strategy definitions remain disabled while permission and historical/session-data gates are blocked. Prospective history qualification accepts only complete intervals bracketed by continuous exact-account witnesses and cannot retroactively qualify old history. A successful software check is not a market return or profitability result. Account limits are not a cross-host ownership fence or a combined portfolio guarantee. The verified single-Demo reconnect does not certify clean-machine setup, second-account broker acceptance or any Live behavior.

The candidate installer is locally self-signed, not publicly trusted. Its enclosed desktop and engine remain unsigned; Windows warnings can remain. Trusted code signing, clean-machine installation and wider broker acceptance remain outstanding gates. Normal downloads continue to resolve only the published non-prerelease channel. Read [release status](releases.html) before choosing a build.
