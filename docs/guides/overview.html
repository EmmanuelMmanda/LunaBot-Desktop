Overview & monitoring

Check your account, active strategies and open trades before starting the day.

Confirm the account#

Use the account selector in the top bar to choose the intended account. Check whether it is Paper, Demo or Live, then read the connection state and latest update.

A disconnected or stale screen can show the last saved values. Wait for a fresh broker update before relying on the position count or balance.

Read the main numbers#

Account equity
The account value including current open profit or loss, as last reported.
Open positions
Positions currently recorded for the account. Pending orders are separate.
Tracked net P&L
Profit or loss over the records LunaBot has tracked. Read the displayed period and coverage.
Drawdown
A fall from previously observed account equity. Stored samples may miss price moves between updates.

Overview in light theme. This example uses simulated Paper funds.

Watch markets and strategies#

Watched markets lists the markets being followed, their quotes and freshness. Active strategies shows each market, timeframe and latest activity.

A strategy may be monitoring without producing a trade. Read its latest activity, then open the strategy to inspect its settings.

Current Scouts shows recent actionable trade ideas. Closed, rejected and expired Scouts are kept in history.

Choose how entries are handled#

Observe only
Watch account and strategy activity without submitting strategy entries.
Review & approve
Review an eligible proposal before approving it.
Automatic
Eligible entries can be submitted under your saved strategy, account and risk settings.

Save mode changes in Settings → Trading mode. Changing the mode does not close existing trades.

Start, pause and Quick Monitor#

The top bar shows monitoring state and its Start or Pause control. Pause new entries prevents new entries while keeping the displayed management behaviour in mind.

Stopping monitoring or the managed MT5 terminal can also stop local trade management. Review Stop LunaBot’s managed MT5 terminal when I pause in startup settings. Broker-held orders and positions can remain open.

Open Quick Monitor from the system tray for account status and quick controls. Use Open LunaBot for the full trading screens.
