Charts & markets

Follow the right broker symbols and arrange charts for the way you trade.

In LunaBotOverview → Charts

Add watched markets#

Open Settings → Markets. Search the broker’s market list and enable the exact symbol you need. Broker suffixes matter: similarly named instruments can be different markets.

Refresh the market list if an expected symbol is missing. Saving a strategy for a market also registers that market for the account.

Open and arrange charts#

  1. Open Overview → Charts.
  2. Select a symbol and timeframe.
  3. Choose the available one-, two- or four-chart layout.
  4. Set each panel’s market and timeframe. Use the chart controls to move, zoom or reset the view.

Check the chart’s displayed candle range. A chart can show a limited part of the available history.

Read prices and spread#

Bid and Ask are the two sides of the quote. Spread is the difference, shown in broker points. Points are defined by the instrument and should not be assumed to mean pips.

Check quote freshness before preparing an order. The latest quote and a closed-candle chart describe different moments.

Timeframes and timezones#

M1, M5 and M15 represent one-, five- and fifteen-minute candles; H1 is hourly and D1 is daily. Strategies evaluate the timeframe saved in their own settings.

Your display timezone changes how dates are shown. It does not automatically change a strategy’s session window. Set that window’s timezone explicitly in the builder.

Move to trading#

Open the trading workspace from Trade Desk to use the chart beside an order ticket. Confirm the account and symbol again before entering terms.

See Prepare a market order.
