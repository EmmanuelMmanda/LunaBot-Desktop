Manual trades & orders

Prepare an order, review its exact terms and follow the broker’s response.

Find the right trading view#

Trade Desk separates Positions, Orders, Proposals, Scouts and Activity. Open the trading workspace for the chart and manual order ticket.

Positions are open trades. Orders are pending broker instructions. Proposals and Scouts are trade ideas or requests that have not necessarily reached the broker.

Prepare a market order#

  1. Confirm the selected account and market.
  2. Choose the Market ticket and select Buy or Sell.
  3. Review Volume · lots, Stop loss and Take profit. Suggested values are starting values; check each one.
  4. Read Bid, Ask and Spread.
  5. Select Preview order.

The preview checks the proposed terms. Preparing or previewing a ticket does not submit the order.

The trading workspace: markets on the left, chart in the centre and order ticket on the right. Paper example.

Prepare a pending order#

Select Pending, choose the supported order type, and enter entry price, stop loss, take profit and expiry.

Buy limit / Sell limit
A limit order requests a price on the favourable side of the current market. Review the broker’s price rules in the preview.
Buy stop / Sell stop
A stop order waits for price to reach the trigger in the order direction.

The broker decides which order types and distances are allowed. A broker-held pending order can fill while LunaBot is closed.

Review and submit#

Read the preview’s account, market, side, volume, entry or quote, protection and risk. If the preview rejects the order, read the reason and correct the relevant field.

Changing a term requires another preview. Submit only when the final terms match your intention, then inspect the resulting order or position.

If the result is uncertain or times out, inspect the original request and refresh broker records before trying again. A second new order can duplicate the first.

Modify or close a trade#

Open a position to inspect its current details and available actions. Review any stop-loss, take-profit or management change before confirming it. Local management plans and broker-held SL/TP are separate.

Open a pending order to inspect, amend or cancel it where supported. Cancellation needs confirmation from the broker; removing an idea or dismissing an alert does not cancel an order.

To exit a position, use its close action and confirm the recorded result. Pausing entries does not close existing positions.

Review proposals and Scouts#

Open a proposal to read its terms and approve or reject it. A Scout’s source assessment is the submitting assistant’s assessment, not a probability of profit.

Current Scouts prioritises active candidates. Use history for rejected, expired or closed items. Inspect the order status after approval; an approval alone is not a fill.
