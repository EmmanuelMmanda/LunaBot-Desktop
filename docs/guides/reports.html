Trading reports

Review trading results, account activity and unresolved orders, then export the period you need.

Choose account and dates#

Select the account, open Report, then set From and To and choose Apply filter. Check the timezone shown below the filters.

Read the reporting period and coverage on the page. An unfiltered account summary and a report for a selected period can show different totals.

Overview#

Start with net P&L, initial account equity, observed drawdown and unresolved orders. The equity chart shows the records available for the selected view.

Cash movements are distinct from trading profit. Stored equity samples do not capture every market movement between updates.

Performance#

Use Performance to inspect trading results and strategy attribution. Compare trade counts, net results and the strategy version attached to those trades.

Trades without a strategy attribution remain separate. A high win rate with few trades is different from the same rate over a longer, well-recorded period.

Review#

Use Review for operational information: connection and sync status, unresolved requests, Scouts and recent warnings or errors.

If an order remains unresolved, inspect it in Trade Desk and refresh the broker record. A green connection indicator alone does not explain an individual order’s outcome.

Export or save a PDF#

  1. Check the account, selected report and date range.
  2. Use Export for Excel for the supported tabular export.
  3. Use Print / PDF and select your printer or the system’s PDF destination.
  4. Open the saved file and check its account, dates and pages before sharing.

Open exports folder helps locate saved exports. Remove account identifiers before sharing a report publicly.

Find an individual event#

Use Audit trail or Trade Desk’s Activity view to inspect a particular action. Use Research reviews when you need to save and check the records around a problem.
