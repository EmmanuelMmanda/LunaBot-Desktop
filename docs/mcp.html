A working connection

More than a market conversation.

01 / Understand

Read your account.

Balances, open trades, orders, strategy activity and recorded results—available to the assistant you connect.

02 / Explore

Turn an idea into a draft.

Words you can use

Start with one good request.

Copy a prompt into your connected assistant. These start in Demo and name the permissions needed.

01

Start the day

Permissions: Account records

Read my shared Demo account in LunaBot. Summarise connection status, balance, equity, open trades, pending orders and strategy activity. Flag stale prices or missing data. Make no changes.

02

Research EUR/USD

Permissions: Account records · Research jobs

Review EUR/USD on my shared Demo account. Check available bars and trading costs. Compare the existing strategies using the same dates and assumptions. Explain drawdown, trade count and limitations. Say if there is too little data to choose. Do not activate a strategy or trade.

03

Prepare a Scout

Permissions: Account records · Trade proposals

Find an EUR/USD setup to review on my Demo account using current available data and my saved limits. Explain entry, stop, target, costs, risk and when the idea becomes invalid. If no setup qualifies, say no trade. Submit a Scout only if my connection has permission; leave execution for my review.

04

Build a strategy draft

Permissions: Account records · Draft strategies

Create a disabled EUR/USD strategy draft using a supported LunaBot strategy type. Explain the timeframe, every parameter, filters, stop and target rules. Use my broker’s exact symbol. Identify any unsupported rule instead of inventing it. Leave the draft inactive for me to review.

05

Preview an order

Permissions: Account records · Manual preview

Help me prepare a protected EUR/USD market order on my Demo account. Ask for any missing side, size, stop or target. Preview the exact order in LunaBot and explain the estimated risk and costs. Do not submit it; show me what I would be approving.

06

Review my trading

Permissions: Account records · Investigations when needed

Review this week’s completed trades on my shared account. Show net results, costs, losses and performance by strategy where available. Explain rejected orders using the recorded reason. Separate facts from guesses and tell me where records are missing. Do not change trades or settings.

Automatic handling
Requires the appropriate Scout policy, execution permissions and compatible account mode. Live trading also requires Live activation. A chat request alone does not enable it.

Ask for a well explained setup, with “no trade” as a valid answer. Neither an AI model nor a backtest can guarantee a profit. Begin in Paper or Demo and learn how orders, stops and risk work.
