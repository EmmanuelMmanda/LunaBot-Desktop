LunaBot 1.0 candidate

Aggressive in research. Deliberate in execution.

The 1.0.0-rc.1 candidate freezes the current desktop and engine together. It is not a production-stable release. The regular Download button continues to use the latest published non-prerelease build.

Review candidate downloads and verification notes ↗

About this download

The candidate installer is locally self-signed and timestamped. Its publisher certificate is not publicly trusted, so Windows and SmartScreen warnings can remain. The enclosed app and engine executables remain unsigned. Do not disable Windows security or install an unknown trusted-root certificate.

The release includes the EXE, an installer ZIP, a manifest and SHA-256 checksums. The extracted engine passed isolated Paper order and research checks; the installer has not completed clean-machine or broker acceptance. Passing these checks does not certify real-money operation.

The desktop

The candidate brings together the trading workspace, expandable charts, account-scoped strategies and order review, reports, connected assistants, and Settings → Operations. Operations uses account tabs and separates connection, monitoring and price freshness.

MT5 setup and reconnection check the selected account and terminal. Existing external terminals require explicit permission before attachment. A successful connection is not proof of fresh prices and does not, by itself, allow new trades.

Research preview

The candidate adds Research → Strategy research: 45 starting hypotheses across EURUSD, USDJPY and XAUUSD. Each explains what it tests, competing explanations and missing requirements. These are research candidates, not 45 trading-ready or profitable strategies.

Saved studies keep their settings, attempt limits and job results. Cancelled or failed attempts still count. A separate synthetic process check tests selection behavior using invented data; its results are not market returns, a win rate, or permission to trade.

Daily-session aggregation and monthly scheduling have code-level tests. Historical clock alignment, complete executable costs and several rule adapters remain incomplete. A blocked strategy stays blocked. No new research candidate has qualified for Live trading.

How to use the preview →

Before production status

  • A trusted publisher signature is not available for this candidate. Do not disable Windows security to install it.
  • Clean-machine installation, broker-specific acceptance and a supervised operating period remain separate release gates.
  • Ten simultaneous broker accounts and cross-host execution ownership are not certified. Do not operate two LunaBot instances against the same broker login.
  • Historical simulations, passing software tests and a high win rate do not establish a profitable trading system.

See the release notes and checksums for the exact artifact. An older build's successful check does not certify a new one.

Update without losing your place

  1. Back up the local database and retain the installer for your current version.
  2. Review open positions and orders before closing LunaBot. Broker-held trades remain open; local management is interrupted while the app is stopped.
  3. Use only the official release assets. The ZIP alternative contains the installer; it is not a portable app.
  4. After an update, verify account identity, reconcile broker records and check price freshness before resuming monitoring.

Do not open a database upgraded by a new version in an older engine. For recovery, restore a compatible backup into a separate location and reconcile before reconnecting. Keep the current data intact until recovery is verified.
